Welcome to Bookdale — our story begins

Hello, and welcome to Bookdale — a new publishing and story development home for fantasy with heart.

We're kicking things off with two titles in active development: Daughters of the Crown, a tale of royal sisters and buried secrets, and Heroes by Accident, the story of an unlikely crew who never asked to be heroes. Both are being developed for publication, and in partnership with the brilliant team at Brightwood Animations, for animated serial adaptations.

On this blog we'll share development updates, world-building notes, behind-the-scenes peeks at the animation process, and news as it happens. Thanks for being here at the very beginning — the best chapters are still to come.
