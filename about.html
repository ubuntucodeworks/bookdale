About Bookdale

Bookdale is a publishing and story development house. We find stories with big hearts and bigger worlds, and we help them grow from first draft to finished book — and beyond.

Right now our shelves are home to two fantasy titles in development: Daughters of the Crown and Heroes by Accident. Both are being developed for publication and for animated serial adaptations in partnership with our friends at Brightwood Animations.

What we believe

Great fantasy isn't about the size of the dragon — it's about the people riding toward it. We champion character-driven storytelling, patient world-building, and authors who write the stories only they can tell.
